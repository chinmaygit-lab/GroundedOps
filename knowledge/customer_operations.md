# Customer operations guidance (synthetic example)

For renewal follow-ups, the customer success engineer checks contract dates, verifies account ownership, and records the next action in the task system. If an invoice mismatch is reported, the billing specialist compares invoice line items with the signed purchase order and requests human review before adjusting a charge.

For onboarding tasks, verify the customer's primary contact, obtain approval for requested access, and share the integration checklist. Access requests must follow least-privilege principles; owners approve elevated permissions.
